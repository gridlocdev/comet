# Comet

A one-button 2D swing game. Hold to latch onto the nearest live anchor and swing round it at a fixed radius; let go to fly off along the tangent. A swing of at least `minArc` boosts speed, scores and builds a chain that multiplies the score (+0.5 every 4, up to ×4); a release aimed at a mote close ahead is a perfect. Hold on for 4 full turns and the tether snaps with nothing for it. Motes give points, speed and extra lives at 3, 7, 12, 18... The deadly edge creeps in from every side, anchors rest longer after use, their reach shrinks, and a hazard bar appears every 30 seconds. Three lives; crash and press to go again. A top-ten table with three initials.

Kept from the first version (Comet2D): every rule and number, and its look: a black field with flat colours, thin lines and a blocky pixel font, nothing glowing, letterboxed on black at 16:9. Left out: its two-player mode and its 3D ring.

The page opens on the title. Pause has Continue, Settings, Restart and Quit to title; the standard settings open from there and from the title. The pause menu and the initials entry are neon cards over the field, not drawn in the pixel font.
