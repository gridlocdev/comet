Each ▶ plays the sound at its mix level and one of the pitches the game uses. The gold "1.0.0" button next to it plays the recording 1.0.0 shipped at the same pitch. The mote ping and the drone are the same as 1.0.0's; the release, crash, swing hum and blips are softer on purpose, to sit with the soundtrack. Loops play for a second.
